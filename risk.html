SAFe · Agile Release Train · Risk Management

Every risk gets a verdict.

ROAM is how an Agile Release Train closes the loop on risk. During PI Planning the train surfaces every risk that threatens the PI objectives, discusses it openly, and assigns exactly one of four states: Resolved, Owned, Accepted, or Mitigated. Nothing is left undecided.

Common mistakes

Everything is Owned
A single tall Owned column means the train dodged the hard conversations. Owned is for risks that genuinely need investigation — not a parking lot.
Accepted without Business Owners
Accepting a risk is a business decision about consequences. If Business Owners weren't in the room, it isn't accepted — it's ignored.
Owner is a team, not a person
"Platform team owns it" means nobody owns it. Write a name.
Mitigated with no plan
Mitigated means a specific action is in the backlog with capacity behind it. No story or spike? It's Owned.
Board dies after PI Planning
The board is a living artifact. A risk that stops being reviewed stops being managed.
Resolved too eagerly
Resolved means the threat is gone — not that the conversation was reassuring.

Dependency tree

Risks that trigger other risks

Each risk can declare parents — the risks it depends on. Roots are risks nothing else blocks; children cascade beneath them. A parent left Owned or Accepted while its children are Mitigated is a warning sign: the mitigation rests on an unresolved foundation.
